# Project ARA 2028 — American Renewal Agenda (and accompanying project white paper).

**A 65-proposal governing platform, drafted into legislation, scored against international evidence, and free for any candidate to run on.**

Produced by the Reform Democrats of the New Commonwealth (RDNC) — a self-funded policy research project operating in the think-tank model. No institutional donors. No corporate sponsors. No party that owns the work.

-----

## What this is

The American Renewal Agenda is a complete governing platform: 65 specific policy proposals across 9 categories, 12 bills already drafted into legislative language, a three-phase sequencing plan that spans 30 years, and a full evidentiary record explaining why each proposal scores the way it does.

It is built to be used. Any candidate may adopt this platform and run on it. No fee. No application process. No institutional relationship required. **The ideas belong to anyone willing to fight for them.**

### Three priority pillars

- **Fix the Foundations** — healthcare, education, infrastructure
- **Same Rules for Everyone** — anti-corruption, accountability
- **Build What Lasts** — domestic manufacturing, energy independence, industrial capacity

### What it scores against

Every proposal carries three independent scores on a 1-5 scale:

- **GP — Global Precedent.** Has this approach been tried and evaluated in comparable nations? Anchored in a 16-nation Tier 1 comparator pool (high-income democracies with rule of law, OECD membership, functional welfare states).
- **UT — US Transition Difficulty.** How achievable is this in the actual US institutional context? Assesses constitutional and legal feasibility, legislative pathway, implementation timeline, US precedent, and synergy with other proposals.
- **B — Societal Benefit.** How significant is the welfare benefit to the broad American population if the policy succeeds? Assesses reach, per-beneficiary magnitude, and distributional progressivity.

Aggregate score on a 1-10 scale is `(GP + UT + B) / 1.5`. Where evidence is ambiguous, the lower score is assigned. Optimism requires substantiation; pessimism does not.

## Where to start

**If you have 90 seconds:** Read the One-Pager (`PUB_ARI03_PublicOnePager_2026_3.pdf`).

**If you have 20 minutes and want the full agenda:** Read the Renewal Agenda (`PUB_ARA_RenewalAgenda_2026.pdf`). All 65 proposals in plain language, organized by category.

**If you are thinking about running on this platform:** Read the Candidate Orientation (`PUB_ARI_CandidateOrientation_2026_6.pdf`). It explains exactly what adopting this platform means, what to say and not say, how to answer the standard attacks, and what to read in what order.

**If you want to evaluate the substance:** Read the Framework — Sourced edition (`REF_RDNC_Framework_v26_Sourced_2026_5.pdf`). Every proposal carries axis-level source citations to peer-reviewed studies, government reports, and international precedent.

**If you want the legislation:** See the bill series — twelve drafted bills covering the core legislative agenda, organized by phase and policy track.

## A note on authorship and method

This framework was not produced by a think tank with institutional backing, a campaign, or a political party. It was conceived and authored by a concerned American citizen working independently, with Claude (Anthropic) assisting in research, structure, and editorial refinement. The judgments, positions, and arguments are the author’s; the methodological infrastructure exists to make them defensible against the same standards applied to comparable work by established research institutions.

Where the agenda discusses AI policy directly (Proposal 30 — AI Safety & Rights Framework), the proposal is held as a policy commitment with bill text deliberately left to be developed by human policymakers. The reasoning is documented in the proposal itself. The same principle has been operationally institutionalized in AIPE Section 9 (Independent Calibration Authority) — the first instance in this bill series of bill-text institutionalization of methodological-ethics commitments.

The work is developed in public view. Critique, fork, adopt, and improve are all encouraged. Attribution to the Reform Democrats of the New Commonwealth framework is appreciated but not required.
